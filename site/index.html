04 / Honest boundaries

A research prototype.
An explicit trust model.

Not production-safe
Read before integrating

A successful pairing check is not authorization to mint. The local pilot gate records an approval after key, signer, threshold and freshness checks. Consumers still need their own action policy and token integration.

What remains open

Single-party setup remains a soundness risk. The delta-only ceremony experiment is incomplete. Off-chain data still needs an authenticated, trusted source.

The pilot validates its instruction format; the legacy verifier retains its demo parser. Collateral bounds rely on the signed-feed model. Production integration still needs hardening and external security review.
